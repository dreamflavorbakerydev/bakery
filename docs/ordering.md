# Customer orders and bake demand

Customers select an assigned active location, a permitted future delivery date, and quantities from their assigned products. Search and plus/minus controls work on phone and desktop. Review saves a draft; submission checks access, product availability, delivery rules, and prices again. Submitted orders cannot be edited by customers. Reorder copies quantities to a new order, uses current prices for its chosen delivery date, and warns about unavailable products.

Prices are resolved for the requested delivery date: a dated customer override wins over the customer's required zone. Wholesale G/H/I prices from the Nelson import remain the selling prices. Retail/return prices are retained in master data. Prices, names, item identifiers, pack sizes, tray capacities, production dates and quantities are saved on order lines. A changed price or product configuration during review blocks submission and asks for a fresh review. Cutoffs are checked again at submission. Late orders following APPROVAL policy are submitted with an approval flag; PROHIBIT policy blocks submission. Repeat submission of an existing order creates no duplicate order or submitted audit event.

The bakery's **Orders** page defaults to all pending orders across all customers, with optional delivery-date filtering. **All orders** includes drafts, cancelled and completed deliveries for historical lookup. **Awaiting approval** lists late submissions requiring review. Open an order to approve/confirm, start preparing, mark ready to pick, mark delivered, or cancel. Status mutations require bakery office access and create order activity records. These controls offer a basic lifecycle, rather than separate picking, loading or invoice workflows.

**Bake totals** groups all outstanding submitted/confirmed/preparing/ready/picking/delivery demand by product and saved pack/tray configuration. Production date defaults to today; choose delivery date grouping when planning a delivery instead. Each product's saved production lead days determine its bake date independently. Dates with outstanding demand provide quick links, including overdue demand. Customer-specific display names do not split identical underlying products. Distinct product records sharing a SKU remain separate.

Ordered quantity × saved pack size gives units to bake. Trays = ceiling(units ÷ saved units per tray). Without a tray capacity the screen shows “Set tray capacity.” Approved/on-time submissions enter the demand totals immediately; unapproved late quantities appear separately. Drafts, cancelled orders, delivered and invoiced orders are excluded. Opening or refreshing the page recomputes demand from orders; there is no forecast or inventory subtraction and no record of units already baked. Aggregate figures are demand requirements, not a promise that all tray spaces will be filled or a record of production completion.

Eric's existing customer record and login are preserved. At implementation time it has no active location, product assignments, or delivery rules. Set those in Administration using actual business choices before ordering. No product or calendar mappings were invented.
