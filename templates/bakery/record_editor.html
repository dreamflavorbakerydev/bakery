GOOD TO KNOW{% if kind == 'customers' %}

A zone is required.

Every customer uses Zone 1, 2, or 3. An active special price for a SKU takes priority over the customer’s zone price.

{% elif kind == 'overrides' %}

Special prices come first.

Choose a product from the customer?s catalog, then set a price and its dates. Customers using the full catalog need no separate product assignment. Expired or inactive overrides automatically return to the assigned zone price.

{% elif kind == 'availability' %}

A catalog of their own.

Only active assigned products appear in the customer’s catalog. VendorID and customer SKU belong to this relationship.

{% elif kind == 'access' and title == 'Login' %}

A login and its access.

Create the login and choose a role. The next screen lets you assign customers to this person.

{% elif kind == 'access' %}

The right access.

Choose a customer for this login. Its customer is determined automatically. Add another assignment to allow another customer.

{% elif kind == 'locations' %}

One customer. One calendar.

Manage the customer contact details and delivery rules together. Pick delivery days, order notice, cutoff time, timezone, and the late-order policy.

{% elif kind == 'products' %}

Keep the source intact.

SKU and UPC are text, so leading zeros stay in place. Ordering packs and units per tray are separate. Prices are maintained in the product’s zone grid.

{% else %}

One reliable record.

Changes are validated before saving and recorded in the audit trail. Deactivate a record to keep its history.

{% endif %}
